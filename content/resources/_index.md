#### Course Notes

##### Accounting
- [Empirical Research in Accounting: Tools and Methods](https://iangow.github.io/far_book/) by Ian Gow and Tongqing Ding.
- [PhD Lecture Notes: Disclosure and Financial Markets](https://ksmith142.github.io) by Kevin Smith.
- [Fundamentals of Financial Accounting Theory: Communication and Markets, with Applications to Theory-Based Estimation](https://sites.google.com/site/jeremybertomeu/ph-d-course) by Edwige Cheynel and Jeremy Bertomeu.
- [PhD Seminars](https://www.robertbushman.net/phd-seminars) by Robert Bushman.

##### Econometrics
- [Applied Empirical Methods](https://github.com/paulgp/applied-methods-phd) by Paul Goldsmith-Pinkham.
- [Metrics Notes](https://about.peterhull.net/metrix) by Peter Hull.
- [Causal Inference for The Brave and True](https://matheusfacure.github.io/python-causality-handbook/landing-page.html).

##### Microeconomics
- [Many textbooks for users in different levels](https://arielrubinstein.tau.ac.il/books.html) by Ariel Rubinstein.

##### Corporate finance 
- [PhD course on Corporate Finance and Financial Institutions](https://finance.wharton.upenn.edu/~itayg/#PhD) by Itay Goldstein.

##### Misc 
- [Finance and Metrics Notes](https://www.charlescywang.com/#) by Charles C.Y. Wang.
